Who you're hiring

Jesse Glenn runs Glenn's Home & Property Repair out of Piqua. He walks every job before it's quoted, and he's on site while the work happens.

Most of what we take on is bigger than an afternoon: a roof, a room, an addition, a house that needs its outside put back together. Whatever the job, you'll know what's happening on it and when.

From the first call to the final walkthrough.

  1. Tell Jesse about the job

    Send the details and any photos you have. Jesse reviews them and discusses a rough range where possible, based on his experience.

  2. See it in person

    Jesse visits the property to check the work and measurements before preparing the written quote. You agree on when to expect it.

  3. Agree on the work in writing

    The written quote and contract set out the scope, materials and payment schedule before work starts.

  4. Work, then walk through it

    Review the finished work together. If the scope or site conditions require a change, the amount is agreed and the contract revised.

A clear conversation about materials

Materials are billed at what Jesse pays, with no markup. You can also supply your own materials; discuss the choices and payment arrangements with him before buying.
